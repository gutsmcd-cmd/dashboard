## 01 Space facts
- Sun: 4.6 billion yrs, yellow dwarf, ~1.3 million Earths by volume — NASA Sun facts https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/ (NASA rounds the age to 4.5 billion)
- Light time ~8 min 20 s — NASA Sun facts (above)
- Jupiter mass and Great Red Spot — NASA Jupiter facts https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-facts/
- Venus ~465 C; 243-day rotation vs 225-day year — NASA Venus facts https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/ ; NSSDC fact sheet https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/venusfact.html ; NASA GSFC https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/690/Venus.html
- Moon recedes 3.8 cm/yr (Apollo retroreflectors) — NASA JPL https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/apollo-11-experiment-continues-to-return-valuable-data/
- Saturn rings reach 282,000 km, main rings ~10 m thick — NASA Saturn facts https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/facts/ ; NASA Cassini rings https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/science/rings/
- Milky Way 100–400 billion stars, ~230 Myr orbit — NASA Sun facts / NASA Milky Way pages
- Scale model (1 m Sun -> Earth 107 m, nearest star ~29,000 km): derived from 1 AU = 107.5 Sun diameters and Proxima Centauri at 4.24 ly

## 02 Brain facts
- 86 billion neurons; ~100 trillion synapses — UCLA BRI https://bri.ucla.edu/brain-fact/billions-of-neurons-trillions-of-synapses/
- 2% of body weight, ~20% of resting energy — Raichle & Gusnard, PNAS 2002 https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC124895/
- Myelinated signals up to ~120 m/s — Current Biology review https://www.cell.com/fulltext/S0960-9822(06)02523-1
- No pain receptors in brain tissue / awake surgery — standard neurosurgery references
- Sleep clears waste (glymphatic flow) — Xie et al., Science 2013
- Corpus callosum ~140–200 million fibres — Tang & Nyengaard stereology, J Anat 2010 https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1469-7580.2010.01333.x ; Jarbo et al. 2012 (~200 million) https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/psychology/cognitiveaxon/documents/Jarbo_etal_2012.pdf
- Hippocampus = Greek for seahorse, memory consolidation — standard anatomy texts
