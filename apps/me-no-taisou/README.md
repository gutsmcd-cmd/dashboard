Eye Rest

English-only eye break for Shirley. One screen per exercise, one big Start button.
The app counts and moves on by itself.

Give her Eye-Rest.html (one file, pictures inside). Open it in Chrome. Nothing to install.

The folder here is the same app with pictures in art/. Do not publish it.

Add ?demo=1 to try a short version. That shortcut is not shown in the app.

Exercises:
- Screen break: 20 minutes of usual work, a Blink cue once a minute, then 20 seconds looking about 6 meters away.
- Near and far: 5 seconds near (finger at 30 cm), 5 seconds far, 10 times.
- Warm hands: warm the hands, cup them over closed eyes without pressing, then let go.
  The screen says this only rests and calms, and does not improve eyesight.
